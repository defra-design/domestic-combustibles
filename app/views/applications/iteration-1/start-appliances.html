Get a stove or other solid fuel appliance certified for use in smoke control areas in England

Use this service to apply to get a stove or other solid fuel appliance certified for use in smoke control areas.

You'll need to provide information about your company and about the appliance.

You can include multiple similar or related appliances in a single application.

Before starting your application, you should review the appliance application guidance, which also contains an application checklist.

Application fees

Fees depend on whether the appliance you are applying for is:

  • unique, or the first to be certified in a range or family of appliances
  • a variant appliance (variants have approximately the same technical design and are covered by the same test report as another appliance)

The certification organisation will calculate and request application fees once they have reviewed your application. Fees are excluding VAT.

Fee for certifying a unique or first appliance
£1,610
Fee for certifying each additional (variant) appliance
£470

Company information

You'll need to provide the following information about your business:

  • company name and address
  • name and email address of a person we can contact if we have questions about your application

Appliance information

You'll need to provide details about the appliance, including:

  • model name and model number, if applicable
  • appliance type (for example stove, boiler or pizza oven)
  • nominal (thermal) output in kilowatts (kW)
  • which fuels the appliance will be authorised to burn

You'll also need to tell us if the appliance is a variant of another appliance that is already certified. If it is, you'll need to provide identifying details for the certified appliance, such as make and model name.

Supporting documents

To support your application, you'll need to provide:

  • test reports that show that the appliance meets legal emissions limits while burning the intended fuel
  • technical drawings of the appliance, including external dimensions, any air control modifications and grate dimensions (if applicable)
  • conformity mark information (CE or UKCA) for the appliance
  • instruction manuals that cover the appliance's installation, operation and maintenance

All supporting documents are only needed to support your application. They will not be made public.

Make sure all supporting documents have relevant file names, so it's clear what they contain. This will make your application easier to process.

Test reports

Review the appliance certification terms and conditions for detailed testing requirements.

You'll need to provide test reports that:

  • show that the appliance meets legal emissions limits while burning the tested fuel
  • are from an ISO 17025 accredited laboratory with scope to test fuel burning appliances
  • include details of testing methodology
  • cover all the appliances included in your certification application

If we don't recognise the testing laboratory, we might ask you for clarification. You can also provide the laboratory's ISO 17025 accreditation certificate along with your test reports.

Technical drawings

You'll need to provide technical drawings that cover all appliances in your application. These must include:

  • front view
  • side view
  • plan (top) view
  • external dimensions
  • any air control modifications
  • grate dimensions (if applicable)

Conformity mark

You'll need to provide information about the European (CE) or British (UKCA) conformity mark of all appliances in your application.

Provide a copy of the information on the data plate, for example in a PDF or photo.

Instruction manuals

You'll need to provide any instruction manuals that explain how to use all appliances in your application, including:

  • installation
  • operation
  • maintenance

Instruction manuals must:

  • be in English
  • contain a note on the requirements of the Clean Air Act 1993

If a manual covers multiple appliances, it must contain a clear statement about which appliances are suitable for use in smoke control areas.

Get more detailed information about instruction manual requirements in the appliance certification terms and conditions.

Applying for multiple appliances

You can include multiple similar or related appliances in a single application.

Similar or related appliances may be variants of each other with approximately the same technical design, or may all be covered by a single test report.

You should submit separate applications for appliances that aren’t similar or related. Putting multiple different or unrelated appliances in a single application will make the application take longer to process.
