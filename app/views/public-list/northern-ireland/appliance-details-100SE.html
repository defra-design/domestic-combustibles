Conditions for use

This appliance is only certified for use in smoke control areas when it is used to burn wood logs or wood pellets.

This appliance must be installed, maintained and operated as instructed in the instruction manual and in any additional conditions for use.

You must only burn wood with a measured moisture content of 20% or less. You cannot burn waste wood that has been painted, coated or treated with chemical preservatives - for example wood pallets, fences or garden furniture.
