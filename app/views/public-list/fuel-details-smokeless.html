Fuel description

Appearance
Pillow-shaped briquettes with a single line indentation on one side and a double line indentation on the reverse side.
Weight
Average weight of between 95 grams to 110 grams per briquette.
Composition
A blend of anthracites and coals up to and between 30% to 40%, pet cokes up to and between 60% to 70% with trace amount of organic plant based dry powder binder as to the remaining weight.
Manufacturing process
Manufactured by a cold cure process using roller press system.
Sulphur content
2% of total dry, ash free weight
