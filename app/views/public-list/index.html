Iteration based on feedback from usability testing sessions with consumers, local authorities and industry specialists.

Content interations and reasoning documented in the design Mural board.

Appliance types and fuel filters were creating confusion for all users, so have been removed.

Country certified filter has been moved out of the detail component, so it is fully visible on screen, to reduce the confusion around what it is and what it does.

Lists reordered to alphabetical first, with numeric ordering after, as the ordering the existing site uses was causing confusion when first looking at the page, with users thinking numeric titled appliances were serial numbers or industry coding.
