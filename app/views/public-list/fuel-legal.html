Legal basis for appliance certification

From: Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

Last updated 14 January 2026

Appliances are certified for use in smoke control areas when they are:

  • on the list of certified appliances
  • only used to burn the allowed fuels
  • used only as instructed by the manufacture

Any appliances that do not appear on this list are not certified for use in smoke control areas.

Appliances are certified for use in each country by that country's government.

England

Appliances are certified for use in smoke control areas in England by the Secretary of State under:

  • the Clean Air Act 1993 (as updated by the Deregulation Act 2015)
  • the Air Quality (Domestic Solid Fuels Standards) (England) Regulations 2020

Scotland

Appliances are certified for use in smoke control areas in Scotland by Scottish Ministers under the Clean Air Act 1993 (as updated by the Regulatory Reform (Scotland) Act 2014).

Wales

Appliances are certified for use in smoke control areas in Wales by Welsh Ministers under the Clean Air Act 1993 (as amended).

Northern Ireland

Appliances are certified for use in smoke control areas in Northern Ireland by the Department of Agriculture, Environment and Rural Affairs under the Clean Air (Northern Ireland) Order 1981 (as updated by the Environmental Better Regulation Act (Northern Ireland) 2016).
