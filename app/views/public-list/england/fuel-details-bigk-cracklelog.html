Fuel description

Appearance
are firelogs that are approximately 235 millimetres in length and 80 millimetres in diameter, with grooves along the faces
Weight
have an average weight of 1.1 kilograms per firelog
Composition
comprise slack wax (as to approximately 55% of the total weight), hardwood sawdust (as to approximately 42% of the total weight) and crackle seeds (as to approximately 3.2% of the total weight)
Manufacturing process
were manufactured from those constituents by a process of heat treatment and extrusion
Sulphur content
have a sulphur content not exceeding 0.1% of the total weight.
