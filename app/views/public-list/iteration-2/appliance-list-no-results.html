Check if a stove or other solid fuel appliance is certified for use in smoke control areas

From: Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

Last updated 14 January 2026

When burning solid fuels (including wood) in smoke control areas, you can either:

  • burn a certified fuel, or
  • burn uncertified solid fuels (including wood) in a certified appliance

Only use fuels that your appliance is designed to burn. Wood fuels should contain 20% moisture or less. Find out more about burning wood at Ready to Burn.

All the appliances on this list are certified appliances. Learn more about appliance certification.
