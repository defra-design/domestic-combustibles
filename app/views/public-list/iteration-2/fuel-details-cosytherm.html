Fuel description

This certification information helps identify fuels that may not be in the original packaging.

Appearance
Are unmarked pillow shape briquettes between L75mm to L80mm x W55mm to W60 x D35mm to D40mm.
Weight
Have an average weight of between 95 grams to 110 grams per briquette.
Composition
Comprise of a blend of anthracites and coals up to and between 30% to 40%, pet cokes up to and between 60% to 70% with trace amount of organic plant based dry powder binder as to the remaining weight.
Manufacturing process
Are manufacture from those constituents by a cold cure process using roller press system.
Sulphur content
2% of total dry ash-free weight.
