This appliance is certified for use in smoke control areas when it is used to burn the following:

Wood logs

Burning wood fuels

Wood fuels should contain 20% moisture or less. Burning wet wood creates more smoke and pollution and less heat.

You cannot legally burn waste wood that has been painted, coated or treated with chemical preservatives. This includes wood pallets, fences and furniture.

Find out more about burning wood at Ready to Burn.
