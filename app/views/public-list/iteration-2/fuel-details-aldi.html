Fuel description

This certification information helps identify fuels that may not be in the original packaging.

Appearance
Are either unmarked pillow-shaped briquettes or, pillow-shaped briquettes marked with a single indented line running longitudinally along each face, off-set from its counterpart by 10 millimetres.
Weight
Have an average weight of either 55 or 80 grams per briquette.
Composition
Comprise anthracite (as to approximately 60 to 80% of the total weight), petroleum coke (as to approximately 10 to 30% of the total weight) and a molasses and phosphoric acid binder (as to the remaining weight).
Manufacturing process
Were manufactured from those constituents by a process involving roll-pressing and heat treatment at about 300°C.
Sulphur content
2 % of total dry weight.
