From: Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs

Published 12 January 2026

Last updated 14 January 2026

This appliance is certified for use in smoke control areas when it is used to burn the following:

Wood based, Wood logs

Burning wood fuels

Wood fuels should contain 20% moisture or less. Burning wet wood creates more smoke and pollution and less heat.

You cannot legally burn waste wood that has been painted, coated or treated with chemical preservatives. This includes wood pallets, fences and furniture.

Find out more about burning wood at Ready to Burn.

How to use the appliance

This appliance must be installed, maintained and operated as instructed by the manufacturer and the certification body:

Installation and operating instructions manual dated 23rd June 2015 reference “JINHCE85 Rev B” and Smoke Control Area Kit Installation Instructions dated 22nd January 2015 reference “JINHCE85SCK revA” Appliance must be fitted with the manufacturer's smoke control kit reference JHCE8501

Contact a local installer or sweep for further advice.
